Color scheme update
- Use these tokens in styles/settings.css (:root):
  --color-bg: #0B1220
  --color-surface: #111A2E
  --color-text: #EAF0FF
  --color-muted: #A9B4D0
  --color-border: #22304F
  --color-accent: #6EE7FF
  --color-accent-contrast: #061018
  --color-danger: #FF5A6A
  --color-success: #37D67A

Rules
- Components must reference semantic tokens only (no raw hex in styled-components).
- Objects/utilities do not get color decisions (layout only).
- Ensure contrast for text on bg/surface meets accessibility expectations (aim for WCAG AA).
- Update interactive states: hover, active, focus ring, disabled.
